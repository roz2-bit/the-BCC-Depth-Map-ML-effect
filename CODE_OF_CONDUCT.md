# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect different experience levels, and keep feedback focused on the work. Do not share private, licensed, or proprietary material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized behavior, and deliberately publishing malware or proprietary assets are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban behavior that violates this code.
